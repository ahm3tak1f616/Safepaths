# Safepaths 2.5.0

Repeated footsteps form paths. Configure road speed globally or per conversion, control creation and decay separately, and optionally prevent new paths while sneaking. Unused paths restore their original terrain. MineColonies support is optional.

Supports **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge** and **Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge**. Choose the download for your version and loader.

## New in 2.5.0

- Separate creation and decay switches, plus an optional sneaking safeguard.
- Optional speed bonuses on conversions and permanent roads. Rules without a speed inherit the global setting.
- Compact settings tabs, source-to-road previews, a searchable block picker, and numeric speed fields with units beside them.
- One save/cancel flow for settings and conversions, with defaults restored from the config specification.
- An operator inspect command for progress, speed, decay, and protection diagnostics.
