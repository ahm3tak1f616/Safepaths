# Safepaths

Safepaths creates trails from repeated foot traffic. Roads give a configurable speed bonus, and unused paths return to their original terrain. MineColonies support is optional.

Supports **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge** and **Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge**. Choose the download for your version and loader.
